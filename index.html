London Worm Club

A community for C. elegans researchers in London

Welcome

Welcome to the London Worm Club! We are a community of researchers working with Caenorhabditis elegans across London and surrounding areas. Our goal is to foster collaboration, share knowledge, and support the worm research community in and around the city.

Meetings

Our main activity is organising meetings 2-3 times per year where worm researchers can present their work, discuss ideas, and network with other members of the community. These meetings are open to all members of the London worm community (and anyone else who can make it along for the day!), and we encourage everyone to attend and participate.
