London Worm Club

A community for C. elegans researchers in London

Welcome

Welcome to the London Worm Club! We are a community of researchers working with Caenorhabditis elegans across London. Our goal is to foster collaboration, share knowledge, and support the worm research community in and around the city.

About Us

The London Worm Club brings together labs and researchers from universities and institutes across London. We organise regular meetings, seminars, and social events for the C. elegans community.

Get Involved

If you are a worm researcher based in London and would like to join our community, please reach out. All are welcome—from PhD students to principal investigators.
