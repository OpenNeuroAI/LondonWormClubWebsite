About

About the London Worm Club

The London Worm Club brings together labs and researchers from universities and institutes across London and the wider area. We organise regular meetings, seminars and social events for the C. elegans community.

Meetings are open to all members of the London worm community — and anyone else who can make it along for the day. Whether you are starting your first worm experiment or running a lab, there is a place for you here!
